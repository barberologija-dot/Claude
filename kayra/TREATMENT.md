# Kayra — treatment

Chinese Ink Wash · 15 s · 1080×1920 (vertical) · 24 fps · Serbian (Latin) inscriptions · no voice · guqin score

## Brief

- Subject: Kayra, born 29 March 2026; now (October 2026, about six and a half months old) she has started crawling and playing with toys.
- On-screen text only, in Serbian; music and foley; no narration.
- A family keepsake for phones: vertical, short, gentle.

## Three candidate structures

1. **One drop, one life.** A single ink drop falls on empty paper and blooms into a curled newborn. The same ink then flows into the crawling child. Birth → growth told as one stroke continuing.
2. **Hanging scroll.** A slow tilt down a vertical scroll: the date, the sleeping newborn, the crawling baby, the toy at the bottom. Stages stacked like generations.
3. **Before / after rhyme.** Two paintings in the same framing: the curled newborn (March), then an ink bloom, and the same spot of paper now holds a baby on hands and knees reaching for a toy.

**Chosen: 1 + 3.** The drop is the hook (a sound and a mark in the first second) and it says "birth" without words. The rhyme of the same framing shows growth, the film's only turn. The hanging scroll is beautiful but too slow for 15 s.

- Opening: **ink first, a single drop** on blank paper, in silence.
- Ending: **one small mark**: her name, written by brush, and the vermilion seal. No pull-back.
- Score shape: unmetred guqin harmonics (newborn, stillness) → a soft pulse that follows her hands (crawling) → one held silence → a bright plucked phrase when she touches the toy → a low open string under the seal.

## Logline and arc

A drop of ink becomes a sleeping newborn, and the newborn becomes a little girl who crawls across the paper to her first toy.

- Setup: a drop, a bloom, a curled newborn. "Rođena 29. marta 2026."
- Turn: an ink bloom carries us six months forward; she is up on her hands and knees, crawling.
- Ending: she reaches the red ball and it rolls; her name is written above her and sealed.

## Benchmark

- *Feelings of Mountains and Water* (1988): learn the empty paper, the patience and the ink bloom transitions. Don't take its figures, landscapes or music.
- Children's picture-book pacing (one image, one sentence, one page turn): learn the tenderness and the single readable action per page. Don't take any book's drawings.

## Cue map

- 0–4.4: unmetred, D minor pentatonic (shang mode on D: D F G A C). Harmonics at 0.9, 1.4, 1.9, 2.5, 3.4.
- 4.4–5.2: zero.
- 5.2–10.0: 84 bpm pulse (beat = 0.714 s), open-string bass on the downbeat; hand landings on the beat (5.2 + n·0.714).
- 10.0–10.9: silence.
- 10.9: phrase (four ascending plucks, 0.12 s apart). 13.1: low open D + harmonic, rings to the end.

- Inscriptions (题跋) in Cormorant Garamond italic, ink colour, no box, a small vermilion square before each line, placed in empty paper; they wet in and dry in about 0.3 s.
  - "Rođena 29. marta 2026." 1.6–4.4 (2.8 s; needed 19/15 + 1.5 = 2.8 s)
  - "Sada puzi…" 6.0–9.6 (3.6 s)
  - "…i igra se." 11.4–15.0 (3.6 s)
- Title "Kayra": brush-written by tracing the font as a skeleton, in the upper empty paper of the last shot, 11.6–15.0; vermilion seal.
- The only red in the world: the ball and the seal, which rhyme.
- No .srt: the inscriptions are part of the picture and there is no speech.
